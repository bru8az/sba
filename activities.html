Beyond The Classroom

Joyful Learning Through
Play & Activity

We believe that education must engage the mind, body, and spirit. Our vibrant extracurricular programs are carefully woven into our scientific pedagogy to build a child's complete foundation.

No Boring Orthodox Methods

Traditional schooling often restricts a child's natural energy. At Satyen Bose Academy, we channel that energy into constructive, character-building activities.

Every activity is designed to complement our core teaching philosophy: breaking concepts down to dust. Through movement, art, and interaction, children unconsciously grasp complex developmental skills in the most natural way possible—by playing.

Our Curated Activities

Yoga & Mindfulness

Cultivating focus, physical balance, and inner calm. Our gentle yoga sessions help young children develop body awareness and teach them early techniques for managing emotions and starting the day centered.

Focus & Balance

Art & Drawing

Encouraging boundless creativity and imagination. Before children can write words, they communicate through art. Drawing helps solidify visual expression and pre-writing wrist control.

Creativity & Vision

Brain-Gym

Specific, scientifically-backed kinetic exercises designed to enhance cognitive function, memory, and bilateral coordination. It literally prepares the brain's neural pathways for academic learning.

Cognitive Agility

Conversation & Fun

We build confidence by giving children a voice. Through guided group discussions, storytelling, and playful banter in English, students overcome stage fright and develop strong social and linguistic skills.

Social & Linguistic Skills

Fine-Motor Training

Before a child can master handwriting, they must master their hands. Our fine-motor training utilizes blocks, bead threading, clay molding, and precise picking exercises. This crucial step strengthens the small muscles in the fingers and hands, directly supporting our foundational goal of making future academic tasks effortless.

Dexterity & Pre-writing
