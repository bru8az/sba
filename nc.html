Narration Mastery

Introduction (ভূমিকা)

Direct Speech: বক্তার কথা হুবহু বলা। (Quoted exact words)

Indirect Speech: বক্তার কথা নিজের মতো করে বলা। (Reporting the meaning)

Direct: He said, "I am busy."

Indirect: He said that he was busy.

1. Tense Change (টেন্সের পরিবর্তন) ▼

If Reporting Verb is in Past Tense (e.g. said), the tense of Reported Speech changes:
(Reporting Verb Past Tense এ থাকলে Reported Speech এর Tense নিচের মতো বদলায়:)

  • Present Indefinite ➔ Past Indefinite I play ➔ I played
  • Present Continuous ➔ Past Continuous am playing ➔ was playing
  • Present Perfect ➔ Past Perfect have played ➔ had played
  • Past Indefinite ➔ Past Perfect played ➔ had played
2. Time & Words (শব্দের পরিবর্তন) ▼

Some words showing nearness change to words showing distance: (কাছের জিনিস দূরের জিনিসে পরিণত হয়)

This ➔ That
These ➔ Those
Here ➔ There
Now ➔ Then
Today ➔ That day
Tomorrow ➔ The next day
3. By Sentence Type (বাক্য অনুযায়ী) ▼

1. Assertive (বিবৃতিমূলক)

Use told instead of said to. Link with that.

He said to me, "I am happy."
➔ He told me that he was happy.

2. Interrogative (প্রশ্নবোধক)

Use asked. Link with if/whether (for Yes/No questions) or WH-word.

He said, "Are you okay?"
➔ He asked if I was okay.

3. Imperative (আদেশ/উপদেশ)

Use ordered/requested/advised. Link with to.

He said, "Open the door."
➔ He ordered to open the door.
