UKG

The Admission Process

We believe in keeping things simple and personal. We prefer face-to-face interactions to understand your child's needs and introduce you to our scientific methodology.

01

Get in Touch

Give us a call to express your interest and schedule a campus visit. We do not use online forms because we believe a conversation is the best way to start a relationship.

Call 9434007236
02

Campus Visit & Counseling

Visit our serene campus in Joykrishnapur. Meet our educators, see our playful learning environment firsthand, and understand how we break concepts to dust. We'll answer all your questions and provide the admission kit.

03

Enrollment

Submit the physical admission form along with necessary documents (Birth Certificate, Photos, etc.) at our school office. Once processed, welcome to the Satyen Bose Academy family!
