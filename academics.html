Our Curriculum

A Journey of Joyful Learning

Every great structure requires a rock-solid foundation. At Satyen Bose Academy, our academic structure is designed to naturally align with a child's cognitive development.

Scientific Pedagogy Meets Play

We strictly avoid orthodox and monotonous teaching methods. Instead, learning happens naturally through carefully crafted playful activities.

By breaking concepts to dust, we ensure that the rudimentary pillars of logic, language, and numbers are ingrained deeply in a child's mind, making future complex learning effortless.

Our Academic Stages

Tailored educational milestones designed specifically for the cognitive capacities of young minds.

Nursery

Age 3+

The Exploration Stage

The critical first step away from home. Nursery is designed to build a sense of security and joy in a structured environment. We focus heavily on sensory exploration and social interaction.

  • Sensory & tactile play
  • Basic language exposure
  • Gross motor skill development

LKG

Age 4+

The Foundation Stage

Lower Kindergarten introduces structured learning gently. Here, we start breaking down phonetic sounds and numerical logic to dust, ensuring children grasp the "why" before the "what".

  • Phonics & basic vocabulary
  • Pre-math concepts (shapes/sizes)
  • Logical reasoning through puzzles

UKG

Age 5+

The Preparatory Stage

Upper Kindergarten prepares students for primary schooling. They transition from play-based learning to structured application, building immense confidence in reading, writing, and arithmetic.

  • Reading fluency & sentence formation
  • Basic arithmetic (addition/subtraction)
  • Environmental awareness
Holistic Development

Beyond The Books

True education engages the mind, body, and spirit. Our integrated extracurricular activities are essential to building the well-rounded humans of tomorrow.

Yoga

Cultivates focus, physical balance, and inner calm to start the day right.

Drawing

Encourages boundless creativity, imagination, and visual expression.

Brain-Gym

Specific kinetic exercises designed to enhance cognitive function and memory.

Conversation & Fun

Building immense confidence through guided social interactions and public speaking.

Fine-Motor Training

Activities to develop hand-eye coordination and finger dexterity for writing.
